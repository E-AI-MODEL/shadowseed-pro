# Pro unresolved-seed longitudinal experiment: protocol delta, NOT an authorized run

**28 October 2026. Status: source-traceable protocol amendment proposal only.**  
**No paid model calls, no independent G0 approval, no claimed Q1/Q2 result.** This document links to, and does **not** replace, the existing [G0 adapter evaluation contract](g0/adapter-evaluation-contract-2026-10-25.md). Project: **Shadowseed Pro**.

## Why the 27–28 October results change our research design

The source-pinned 27 October data have 48 original detector turns and 94 candidate occurrences. The historical form-based intake allowed 48 true births and excluded 46. The PR #262 mechanical four-turn replay allowed 48 bounded plus **46 unresolved** real weightless births (all retained, no authority or normal-answer influence). This was a **replay of frozen host and detector output**; the source contains no independently demonstrated second-order relational development. The 46 historical exclusions had 78 later turn-windows in their respective sessions, **not 78 observed new relationships**.

What was lost was **opportunity to follow unbounded candidates through Pro memory**, not proven conceptual novelty. The 46 are not a new score denominator for a "good seed" success rate.

## Nonnegotiable separation of actors and events

Each event must be timestamped, source-hashed and attributed to an actor:

1. Ordinary **host LLM** generates an answer with no unapproved seed context.
2. Existing Pro **detector** reads that answer (and its authorized prior history), proposes a raw candidate. Original raw text survives.
3. Existing Pro **intake** creates either a bounded seed, an unresolved seed or an audit-only parser observation. The latter is never relabelled as born.
4. **Pro memory** follows that *specific* ID through trace, same-turn/no-credit, exact recurrence, TTL/DORMANT/TrTL and expiry. No silent semantic rewrite. No Gate influence without authority.
5. A **separate research-only investigator** can at a preregistered checkpoint inspect a frozen read-only projection of the seed and subsequent evidence. This is an **intervention** with its own tokens, calls and provenance, never a spontaneous change to the normal host.
6. Later questions, tool uses and feedback are recorded with authorship (**host, detector, researcher-side model, human, user, external world**). A proposed relation never becomes model-original by copying it into a prompt, external corpus or assessor key.
7. Independent evaluator tests the meaning and actual subsequent investigability **after outputs are frozen**. No value or atomicity criterion filters initial birth.

**Key causal fact:** If all candidate memory is weightless and never consulted by any component that can propose later questions, a D-vs-E difference in later normal-host generations **cannot be attributed to that memory**. We cannot call an inert, stored record an autonomous reasoning influence. A research-only observer is one possible *explicit* inquiry mechanism; its results must be compared to matched alternative observers and cannot be smuggled into ordinary answer generation.

## Prospectively registered stages (after G0)

- **T0 / Q1:** In equal-budget host generations on a separately protected scenario, characterize what normal high-capability generation selects. Freeze full outputs **before** running detector. A candidate's absence from finite outputs does not show impossibility or hidden-neuron absence.
- **T1 / birth:** Run Pro detector. Retain all parsable, context-attributed possibilities including unresolved ones without judging their content. Record IDs and immutable origin/provenance. Do not re-use the human review packet as model input.
- **T2 / life:** Let subsequent real world/user observations and ordinary host turns occur with no unapproved candidate in the normal host prompt. Continue the **same** Pro engine session, not a one-turn prompt with all stages concatenated.
- **T3 / frozen investigation checkpoint:** Run a **separately budgeted and logged** research-side inquiry. In the Pro arm, the observer may be shown the candidate **once at this checkpoint**, not repeatedly at T1 and T2. That is a deliberate memory-reading intervention, NOT autonomous host discovery. In controls, the observer gets an equal-budget ordinary note, deliberate search opportunity or exact-candidate ablation/sham.
- **T4 / dependent question and genuine feedback:** Observe an independently testable next inquiry and its actual feedback in a general executor. Distinguish an independently warranted, meaningfully new question from a paraphrase, familiar planning step, new external information, or extra model-call effect. Record no-opportunity, unsupported tool requests, ambiguous relation and unchanged inquiry as real outcomes.
- **T5 / review:** Bind model, question, source, observation, actual tool feedback and candidate ancestry. Blind independent semantic review before arm unblinding.

## Arms and budget

The source [G0 comparison contract](g0/adapter-evaluation-contract-2026-10-25.md) remains controlling:

| Arm | Specific mechanism / read-only checkpoint | Dominant confound to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Ordinary | Same capable model, ordinary generation, no shadow memory | Strong same-model baseline |
| B Deliberate | Same model allowed divergent/search reasoning with same budget | Ordinary search capacity |
| C Ordinary notes | Equal-budget long-context/plain-notes memory | Mere persistence |
| D Pro weightless | Actual `prepare_turn -> ordinary host -> observe_turn`, bounded/unresolved seeds, frozen read-only checkpoint | Whether actual Pro persistence matters |
| E Ablation/sham | Same D mechanics but precommitted specific seed removed or swapped; preserve byte/token budget | Candidate-specific effect vs generic reminder |

**Equal ceilings, not necessarily equal realized usage:** count every host generation, detector, research observer, retry, parser truncation, tool request, output token and world feedback. An absent candidate, expired seed and unused budget remain in the originally assigned denominator. A shorter or longer output is not "better" solely by length.

**No semantic filtering at birth.** If an unresolved candidate is composite, leave it intact and label that **scope uncertainty**. It may later branch into individually traceable bounded hypotheses under a new, explicit source-linked ID; the old seed must not be edited into the resulting claim.

## Two regimes, never pool

- **RF fixed-feedback (supporting control):** all arms receive the same precommitted external observation sequence; examine differential later questions/interpretations without claiming arm-specific interventions changed the world.
- **RI adaptive independent inquiry (primary only if the evaluator passes G0):** arms may choose different admissible actions and receive each action's true feedback in independent, identically initialized worlds. Compare shared constraints and allowed budgets, **not forcibly identical subsequent observations**.

Both need two independently verified different mechanism families, hidden new confirmatory cases, negative cases and an executor that can assess unexpected questions without mistaking "unsupported by parser" for "no opportunity".

## Predetermined failure and negative results

- No original candidate, no natural repeat, no meaningful relation change, no successor question, TTL expiry, unsuccessful/unsafe inquiry, executor failure and evaluator uncertainty all stay visible.
- A/B/C match or outperform D at the same budget: **no established incremental contribution of Shadowseed** in that condition.
- D and E differ only because D's prompt includes the seed: at most a candidate-*conditioning* effect, not proof that weightless storage autonomously changed the model's possibility space.
- Any evidence that the assessor authored the novel affordance or later question must be attributed externally, not to the seed.
- E matches D after ablating the specific candidate: candidate-specific causation unsupported.
- No conclusive association in a small number of correlated runs: neither acceptance nor refutation of the general philosophical possibility.
- A hypothesized later relation appears in a normal control: it is **not unique to D**.
