## What is currently verifiable

The same original archive was independently counted twice, including SHA-256 file integrity, 62 Actions ZIP archives with 145 nested files (114 unique byte hashes), 196 fixed22 candidate occurrences, 94 dual-model candidate occurrences and the intake replay. Two code paths agree on the current English intake admitting 189 of the original 196 fixed22 candidates and the historical Dutch dual-model run rejecting 46 of 94. The recorded review CSVs contain **83 labels** (57 helpful, 13 neutral and 13 harmful), but provenance and blinding varied; those labels are not 83 independent human trials.

Both fixed22 no-SSL and SSL arms are retained, but the SSL arms report **zero seed promotion and zero surfacing**. The prospective answer-effect hypothesis must not be read from differences between those no-exposure arms. The 27 October dual-model run yielded ten complete A/B trajectories but zero completed independent human reviews. One exact-deletion run has 16 responses in four conditions, all in one case. These observations support a methods-first exploratory synthesis, not a pooled effect size or the claim that the original two-part philosophical thesis is already demonstrated.

## Independent interpretation review — prepared, not completed

The author-controlled **restricted review materials** sample 120 candidate observations using recorded model and intake strata, of which 80 fixed22 items also have one ordinary same-turn no-SSL answer. The item order is randomized per reviewer; source model and verdict are withheld. Separate unblinding information remains off-repository.

These are retrospective known cases, **not sealed prospective holdouts**, and have **zero newly completed human ratings** at the time of this snapshot. Before making claims about deep/valuable or not-ordinarily-selected candidates, two human researchers should independently assess the packets without consulting the key or the original analysis. Do not treat an AI review as an independent human verdict. Baselines with one ordinary sample per turn cannot support an exhaustive absence claim.
